Where the numbers come from. YouTube channels come from a crawl of YouTube search on 2026-09-18: 35 firm names crossed with review, discount code, payout, challenge, passed and vs, plus generic terms in nine languages, paginated to hundreds of results each. Reach figures come from each channel's own page, from two similar-channel exports seeded on Blue Edge Financial and Trade With Jem, and from the named affiliates sweep of 17 firms. A dashed firm chip means the channel surfaced when searching for that firm; a solid chip means the firm is named in the bio or a code was found. Discord and Telegram member counts are read from the invite and preview pages. Reddit blocks unauthenticated reads, so it is not in this version. View rate is average views per video divided by subscribers; it is the closest public proxy for engagement. Recurring viewers and true engagement rate are only visible inside a creator's own YouTube Studio, so ask for a screenshot once you are in talks. Instagram, X, Discord, Telegram and TikTok are recorded as links only so far; follower counts for those still need collecting. Prop firm codes are read from video descriptions and channel links on the date checked. Outreach status and notes are saved to this page's database and shared with everyone who can open it.
